FleetSheet v1.1 — pack 2026-10-26x (build 'x')
================================================

Jason's one-ticker ruling (2026-10-26): the Today ticker is
the only ticker in the build. It lists everything, it is
filterable, it shows five items, and it is collapsible with
its state kept. Every other ticker is wiped out. His
NOTHING OFF THE RANK ruling the same day: everything must
be in the rank somewhere — nothing in the system is off the
rank, ever; an item may stand very low, but if the operator
punches through everything above it there is nothing left
that can be done. Judged in the factory-default state (all
switches on, all categories on, nothing snoozed): a user's
own toggles are his acts and are respected; they are never
the excuse for a design exclusion.

1. ONE TICKER, ON TODAY ONLY. The shared strip is fed only
   on the Today page. The Billing feed (all tabs) and the
   Reports feed are removed, and the Assets page's own
   ticker is removed whole — the page keeps its kind bar,
   Latest Changes, Add Asset, and View door. No page other
   than Today renders ticker markup. The page shell is
   otherwise unchanged: pages that never showed a strip are
   byte-stable.

2. CAPTURE ALL. Today's pool is one union: the shipping-paper
   lines, the disputed and past-due invoice taps and the
   PO-expiry taps, the open-ended-rental contention lines,
   the compliance groups, and the five Assets families
   (unit conflicts, live purchase orders, accepted quotes,
   expected off-rent returns, a sole unit unfit for work),
   merged with the top-of-day items. Rank ladder (stakes,
   high to low): shipping paper; disputed; conflicts;
   past-due and PO expiry; contention; live POs; compliance
   groups; accepted quotes; off-rent; sole unit; idle-unit
   paper. Filter categories: shipping paper, compliance
   groups, sole-unit and idle paper are compliance;
   contention, conflicts, accepted quotes and off-rent are
   rentals; disputed, past-due, PO expiry and live POs are
   money. Module switches still win over categories exactly
   as before. One invoice, one tap stands.

3. FIVE ITEMS. The strip shows five items before the
   "+ N more" line; the engine cut and the render cut agree
   at five. The overflow keeps its workload semantics — the
   total counts everything, a grouped compliance line weighs
   its item count.

4. NOTHING OFF THE RANK. The Assets families' old rule —
   paper surfaces only when a job is coming soon — does not
   survive the merge. A new bottom family lists certification
   paper on idle units: an active unit with no open rental
   and an operative certification expired, or due within
   30 days, stands at rank 10, counted in the workload total
   and reachable by punching through everything above it.
   The one hard producer limit found in the pool (live POs,
   ten rows) is removed. A full producer census — every
   condition that can hold an item back, with its
   disposition — stands in PHASEX-REPORT.md; the genuine
   boundary cases (per-shift checklist rows, not-yet-due
   items, the PO-expiry window) are flagged there for Jason,
   not silently settled.

5. WHAT STAYS. The Filter popover governs the whole pool.
   Snooze stands on every shown row with the standard
   stepper. The calm line stands on a quiet book. The
   collapse keeps its state (the ticker:<path> store now
   only ever carries "/" — leftover keys for other paths in
   an operator's browser are inert; no cleanup code).
   Single spacing throughout.

6. MANUAL. Tree USER_MANUAL.txt updated to the one-ticker
