FleetSheet v1.1 — pack 2026-10-25u (build 'u')
================================================

Jason's ruling (2026-10-25): universal summary views. Every
document gets a summary view titled "Summary of Rental#
XXXX", "Summary of Sale# XXXX", "Summary of Work Order#
XXXX", etc. — Billing documents too. "The work stays on the
forms and in the forms' popups." The summary is the third
universal pattern, after the form and the popup. This
resolves build 'r''s JC-1 remainder.

1. ONE SUMMARY PATTERN FOR EVERY DOCUMENT. Rental, Sale,
   Work Order, Quote, Change Order, Invoice, Payment, and
   Credit Memo each open on a summary titled exactly
   "Summary of <Kind># <number>". The facts are stated the
   same way on each: customer, date, status, the document's
   lines, its totals, its notes, its Job Info values, and its
   record stamps (who and when it was entered, overrides,
   confirmations, out-of-flow marks). A fact that does not
   apply, or carries no value, is not stated. The Work Order,
   Quote, Change Order, Payment, and Credit Memo summaries
   are new; the rental ticket page, the sale detail, and the
   invoice view page became the pattern.

2. THE WORK COMES OFF THE SUMMARIES. No working section
   stands on a summary; each kind's work rides behind popup
   doors in one door row, with Edit (where a form exists),
   Print (the document's three-choice print), and the
   universal View at the bottom left:
   - Rental: the money panel is a Payment popup; Check-Out
     and Check-In are popups (same fields, same routes, same
     gates, Proceed-anyway unchanged); the status block
     states Now and Next Step as facts, and the act lives in
     a Status popup; Move with note is its own popup; the
     Job Info popup stands as build 's' left it; the stored
     charge lines stand on the summary as its lines, read
     only, under their record caption. The Work Ticket and
     Delivery Ticket print doors stand in the door row.
   - Sale: the money panel is a Payment popup.
   - Work Order / Quote: their money panels are Payment
     popups — the standing panels PHASET named as JC-T4 are
     gone from their forms. The Work Order's quote-expiry
     panel states as a fact line on its summary.
   - Invoice: the payment form is a Payment popup; the line
     work (Add a Line, the per-line note and delete forms)
     is an Edit Lines popup; the PO form is the Job Info
     popup; the paperwork slots are a Paperwork popup. The
     lines table on the summary itself is read only.
   - Payment and Change Order carry no Edit door: neither
     has an edit flow, and a posted record's fields never
     change. A Credit Memo's Edit door opens the Credit Memo
     form for the memo's customer, where its application
     work lives.

3. RECORD LINKS LAND ON SUMMARIES. Every link that opens an
   existing document — the View lists, Today, the Done
   record links, the ticker, the audit pages, and the
   references one document makes to another (invoice to
   ticket, quote to its documents, a payment to the documents
   it settled) — opens the summary. Only Edit doors and
   creation flows open forms. Post handlers that return the
   operator to a document return to its summary.

4. ONE DEFECT FOUND AND FIXED IN THE SCREENSHOT PASS. The
   invoice's Payment popup stood open on page load: built by
   hand to lead with a Close span, it was absorbed by the
   shell's section wrapper into the Payments & Credits box,
   whose expand restore cleared its hidden state. It is now
   built with the standard popup wrap, as every other popup
   is; the #record-payment anchor stands. Two smaller
   finishes ride with it: summary fact rows with no value no
   longer render as labeled blanks, and the summaries' door
   row aligns its doors on one baseline. The Change Order
   save defect (PHASET D-T1) is NOT touched; it awaits
   Jason's ruling, as before.
