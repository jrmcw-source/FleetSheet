Build 'ab' carries out Jason's rulings of 2026-10-26 evening:

1. VOID-AND-REVISE, ALL EIGHT DOCUMENT KINDS (Rental, Sale,
   Work Order, Quote, Change Order, Invoice, Payment, Credit
   Memo). A document is a draft only while never saved AND
   never printed. Editing a posted/issued document never
   updates it in place: the form opens LOCKED (the record —
   viewable, printable); its Edit button opens the revise
   popup (popup-law form popup) asking for a Reason
   (required) and the operator Name (required, prefilled
   with the desk user); confirming VOIDS the original with
   reason + name + timestamp signed into the record and
   creates the revision in the same engine operation
   (JC-AB0, confirm-time), prefilled from the original's
   full content, its number prefilled "<original> Rev. N"
   in a free-text field. Save posts the revision. The chain
   links by engine key ID, never by number; uniqueness runs
   over the kind's whole history INCLUDING voided documents
   (a voided number stays taken). The newest revision is
   the live document: registers show the head, money reads
   resolve through the chain, summaries render the chain
   both directions ("Revised by <number>" + reason + name
   on the voided original; "Revision of <number>" on the
   revision). A bare save against an issued document
   without a revision session is refused, pointed at Edit.

2. THE THREE EDITORS. Rental: the booking form loads the
   rental (route /ticket/new?kind=rental&edit=<number>, the
   invoice editor's idiom); the summary's Edit door targets
   it. Sale: the sale form loads the sale's group by engine
   key; gates — a revision's total may not drop below what
   the chain has settled, Plant/Fleet confirmations still
   govern, invoiced lines refuse. Payment: the correction
   model the brief specified, aligned to the same Rev. N
   chain — NO payment void/reversal machinery existed in
   the engine before this build (grep-verified); it was
   built new (void_payment_batch signs both ledgers and the
   line allocations; the replacement posts through the
   ordinary payment paths under the minted batch number).
   Five payment edit blocks are named judgment calls
   (JC-AB7..JC-AB11): already-void batches, credit-kind
   rows, cash-settled payments (the cash entry is the
   record), money carried onto an invoice, and batches that
   issued an overpayment credit memo. A correction whose
   document was itself revised posts its split against the
   chain head (JC-AB26).

3. CASH + PETTY CASH INSIDE THE MODEL (redirect #2).
   Pre-build facts: cash entries carried NO operator-facing
   number (the register's first column showed the raw
   engine key); petty entries carried a system-minted
   PC-#### (shown in the drawer header, not operator text).
   Now: cash entries carry entry_no (free text, generated
   default CE-####, unique across the register including
   voided — JC-AB21, a ruling-derived addition), and
   petty's pc_id IS its operator number (free text at
   creation, prefilled with the peeked mint). Both registers
   gain Edit doors in their own idiom and the full
   void-and-revise flow behind them; a settling cash entry's
   revision releases its settlement and re-posts on Save.
   Petty stays a separate log. The drawer header's
   Next-entry figure now peeks instead of minting on every
   render (JC-AB27), so the header and the form agree.

4. COMPANY SNAPSHOT AT ISSUANCE (redirect #3). Every
   issuance snapshots the company identity (name + the
   header's address/contact fields) onto the document row.
   The five printpack document surfaces — invoice, credit
   memo, quote, change order, rental Work/Delivery Tickets
   — print the SNAPSHOT, never the live Company record; a
   posted revision snapshots the identity current at ITS
   posting; the voided original keeps its own. Register and
   report prints are current-data prints, not document
   reprints, and keep the live header (JC-AB24). LEGACY:
   documents issued before this build carry no snapshot and
   print from the live Company record — named for Jason's
   ruling at his look; no audit-trail reconstruction was
   built. Work Order, Sale and Payment have no printpack
   document print (page prints only), as before.

5. THE UNIVERSAL FORM ROW. One row, split, on every form:
   LEFT Add Item, Job Info, Edit (Edit only on a locked
   posted form); RIGHT Print, Save — top-commit forms
   (Payment "Apply payment", Cash In/Out, the petty cards)
   keep their commit in the RIGHT cluster with Print, and
   the Payment scope toggle stays beside it (JC-AA2
   stands).
