FleetSheet v1.1 — pack 2026-10-25t (build 't')
================================================

Jason's 'r' look (2026-10-25), items 2–5. The governing law,
stated the same day: "no form, no info below the line item
row(s) and the totals. the forms are universal. and so is the
pattern for things not on the form." A form's information has
four homes — header zone, the one line grid, Job Info (popup),
Notes — and anything that is not form content uses the one
off-form pattern: a popup.

1. INVOICES OPENS TO A BLANK INVOICE. Billing → Invoices
   renders the blank invoice editor in place, the way Payments
   and Credit Memos land on their blank forms. This SUPERSEDES
   build 'n''s landing on the most recent invoice. Every "New
   Invoice" door is removed — the money picture's card and the
   invoice view page's link (the editor's edit-mode door went
   in build 'r') — the tab is the door, and existing invoices
   open through the View list. One link stands, and it is not
   a door: the Payment form's "→ New Invoice" forward link on
   its no-open-invoices branch.

2. EVERY DOCUMENT FORM'S HEAD IS TWO ROWS. Row 1: Company
   upper left; form # (larger and bold) + Date upper right.
   Row 2, below the bar: Customer left; the Job Info box right
   — navy border, "Job Info" heading, the form's own Job Info
   values with a dash where unset, display only (the popup
   edits). Invoice (new + edit), Credit Memo, and Work Order
   were the named scope; Sale, Rental, Quote, and Change Order
   take the identical pattern. Measured: head height 247px,
   line band at a 255px offset from the head's top, on every
   document form; the Job Info box carries a fixed height so
   no form sits higher than another. The invoice form itself
   changed where it differed from the enumeration: its
   customer block stood in the middle column and it carried
   no Job Info box — both are corrected, not matched around.
   The Credit Memo form's stray "Invoice Ref pulls lines."
   hint paragraph is removed from the form's flow.

3. BELOW-THE-LINES CLEANUP, ALL DOCUMENT FORMS. Fields that
   stood below (or above) the line rows now ride in the form's
   Job Info popup, same names, saved with the form: Credit
   Memo — Invoice Ref, Inv Date, Reason, Upload; Work Order —
   Uploads; Sale — Condition; Quote — Valid Until, Against,
   Site (restored: the field was built but never rendered,
   and a quote's site was silently cleared on edit-save),
   Input by (read-only), and the linked-documents block
   (read-only); Rental booking — On-Rent, Off-Rent Est,
   Transport, Deliver to, Upload. The rental booking's summary
   strip moves below the line rows (the totals zone), hidden
   while empty. Credit Total and quote Total stand with their
   totals zones; Notes stand everywhere. The Credit Memo's
   Inv Date is a reference display only (the referenced
   invoice's date); it never persisted — the memo's date is
   the head's Date.

4. WORKING SECTIONS RIDE IN POPUPS. (a) The Plant Sale /
   Fleet Sale confirm + signature come off the Sale form:
   saving such a sale opens a Confirm sale popup holding the
   same checkboxes and a Confirm & Save button. The audit
   stamps ("Plant sale confirmed." / "Fleet sale confirmed.")
   and the engine's refusal without confirmation are
   unchanged, proven in both directions. (b) The Credit Memo
   Apply Leftover form stands in a popup behind an Apply
   Leftover button. (c) The Quote's status and convert forms
   stand in their own popups behind Status and Convert doors.
   The ticket page's working sections are untouched (Jason's
   open look item).

5. ONE DEFECT FIXED. The Credit Memo form crashed for any
   picked customer (its Documents query selected a column
   that does not exist); the surface — Apply Now, Documents,
   Apply Leftover — was unreachable in build 's'. Fixed and
   pinned by test. One defect FLAGGED, not fixed: the Change
   Order form cannot save (its handler and engine disagree —
   invoices vs quotations, and no Reason field); it awaits
   Jason's ruling. See PHASET-REPORT.md, D-T1.
