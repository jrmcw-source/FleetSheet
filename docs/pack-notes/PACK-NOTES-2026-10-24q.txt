One defect, fixed as a class, under Jason's ruling of 2026-10-24
23:18: "no job site isn't uncommon, even in the oilfield world,
much less my grandma's. it shouldn't block anything, if it's blank
there's just no tax location to calculate, at worst, and that's
not our ballpark in the first place."

THE DEFECT (found in 'p', pre-existing): a ticket booked with no
site (site_id NULL — booking is deliberately site-optional) could
not render its own page and its money came back empty.

THE FIX — blank site is a normal state of a ticket:
- engine ticket_money: the site join is a LEFT JOIN; a site-less
  ticket's money computes in full, site-derived fields stand
  empty, and tax comes only from the ticket's own explicit
  jurisdiction (no jurisdiction = no tax, the operator's call).
- Ticket page: the header states the site as "Site —" when blank.
- Ticket print pack (Work Ticket / Delivery Ticket sheets) and
  the invoice print fallback: LEFT JOINs; sheets render.
- Stays report: LEFT JOIN; a site-less ticket appears with its
  site stated blank instead of vanishing from the report.
- Quote conversion: the "set a site before converting" gate is
  removed — a site-less quotation converts, and the tickets it
  books carry no site.
- Manual §5.4 and §11.5 state the rule in terms.

Checked and already safe (unchanged): the invoice Ship To block
(no site = no Ship To prints, by design); the invoice job box
(already a left join); the health check for a site assigned to a
different customer (a site-less ticket has no site customer to
mismatch); Today/ticker/reports money (no site joins — they ride
ticket_money); booking itself (site-optional since 2026-10-07).

Booking stays site-optional. No site was made required anywhere.
