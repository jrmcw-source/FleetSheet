FleetSheet v1.1 — pack 2026-10-25s (build 's')
================================================

One item. Jason's look at build 'r' (2026-10-25) flagged the
box below the form on the rental ticket page — the Transport
and Possession form carrying the ticket's charges line-item
grid — and ruled: "it's unnecessary and breaks every rule we
have. a line item entry on the invoice can be freight, can
have a description, can be taxed or not, any tickets needing
uploaded can go in the job info popup. please get rid of it."

1. THE FORM IS GONE. The rental ticket page renders no
   Transport and Possession section and no charges grid. The
   page keeps its header/info, billed mark, summary cards,
   money panel, condition forms, status form, Move-with-note
   form, print links, and the View row. The booking form's
   charges grid is gone with it: stored ticket charge lines
   are typed nowhere.

2. CHARGES ON TICKETS END AS A CAPABILITY. A ticket's total
   is its computed lines (rental, damage waiver, environmental
   charge). An invoice built from a ticket carries the
   computed lines only; freight and extra charges are typed
   on the invoice by the operator, each line with its own
   description and Tax flag. Ticket money is blanket-only:
   the per-line settlement paths for tickets are retired
   (engine.doc_line_states returns no lines for a ticket), and
   a settlement aimed at a stored line folds to blanket when
   the ticket is invoiced.

3. TRANSPORT LIVES IN THE TICKET'S JOB INFO POPUP. A Job Info
   button stands where the form stood, opening the ticket's
   Job Info popup (the document-form popup pattern): Transport
   (haul_by), Deliver to, Tax Jurisdiction (same combo /
   hidden-id mechanics), the possession/tax note and haul
   consequence under the fields, and a file upload for ticket
   paperwork (the existing job-attachment model; files land in
   docs/ named ticket_<id>_...). The popup saves through the
   repurposed /ticket/<id>/transport route. The delivery
   ticket print reads the same fields as before. A locked
   ticket (billed, closed, void) renders the popup read-only
   with the lock reason stated, and the route refuses the
   save — the old form's lock rules, unchanged.

4. EXISTING STORED LINES — CENSUS AND DISPOSITION. Census
   before any edit: demo.db holds 4 stored ticket_lines, all
   on UNBILLED "On Rent" tickets — T-1001: Freight $150.00
   untaxed + Fuel $45.00 taxed ($195.00); T-1002: Freight
   $125.00 untaxed + Fuel $100.00 taxed ($225.00). T-1003
   (billed) holds none; blank.db holds none. Under the record-
   is-the-protection rule nothing was deleted and no total
   silently changed: those lines stay counted in their
   tickets' totals and are listed read-only in the Job Info
   popup (listed, not editable, no Add/Delete). Per-ticket
   totals on the demo book are byte-for-byte the same figures
   before and after (T-1001 $778.62, T-1002 $1,287.54,
   T-1003 $1,470.94). The ticket_lines table stays.
