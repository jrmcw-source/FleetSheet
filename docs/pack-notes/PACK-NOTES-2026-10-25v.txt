FleetSheet v1.1 — pack 2026-10-25v (build 'v')
================================================

Jason's rulings (2026-10-25): "No form number is ever a key
ID. Key ID's must exist for every table, always be unique,
and assigned by the engine." A Change Order is a standalone
money document — "Nothing requires anything." And never the
bare word "ticket" — qualified names only.

1. EVERY TABLE IS KEYED BY THE ENGINE. Each table now
   carries its own engine-assigned key ID as its primary
   key. A document's number (Invoice#, Rental Ticket#,
   Work Order#, Quote#, Change Order#, Credit Memo#), a
   customer's C- number, a unit's A- number, a site's
   S- number — these are display numbers: unique, permanent
   once posted, and exactly what the operator types, sees,
   and prints, everywhere as before. They are no longer the
   tables' keys; the key and the number are separate things
   for separate reasons. Books written by earlier builds
   migrate on first open, with no operator action; every
   document, line, allocation, total, and stamp carries
   over unchanged (proven by a before/after dump comparison
   on the demo book: counts and money totals identical, the
   standing spot figures exact). Sale numbers are minted one
   way now (S-<year>-<n>); numbers already issued never
   change.

2. THE CHANGE ORDER IS A STANDALONE MONEY DOCUMENT. It
   needs no quote and no other document. It carries its own
   number, customer, lines (adds and subtracts), reason, and
   total. It may name ONE associated money document —
   Invoice, Sale, Work Order, Rental, or another Change
   Order — stated as a fact on both documents' summaries;
   the association changes no totals anywhere. A quote no
   longer absorbs change order amounts: a quote's numbers
   are its own, and a changed quote is reissued as a new
   quote ("Quote# XXXX rev 1"). A change order is settled
   on itself, like any receivable: payments allocate
   against it from its summary or the payment form, in any
   tender, at any time. One payment may settle an invoice
   and its associated change order in a single event, with
   the amount stated per document (the amended-PO case).
   Open change orders stand in the money picture alongside
   the other receivables, and an open change order past its
   date appears in Today's money like any other open
   document. Once money has settled against a change order
   its lines stand as issued; a correction is a new change
   order.

3. EQUIPMENT TAB ORDER. The form strip reads, left to
   right: Sale, Rental, Quote, Work Order, Change Order,
   Check In/Out. Change Order is the last of the money
   forms. Assets stays far right, alone at the edge,
   unchanged.

4. NAMES SAY WHAT THEY MEAN. The bare word "ticket" is
   gone from operator-facing text, in the app and in the
   manual: the documents are Rental Tickets; the two papers
   keep their names, Work Ticket and Delivery Ticket; the
   reference field is Reference Ticket. Job Info attachments
   now leave their event in the Done record (before this
   build the record silently failed); attachments keep
   resolving by their document number, which never changes.

The manual (USER_MANUAL.txt) is updated for the change
order chapter, the tab order, and the naming sweep. The
printed manual (manual.pdf) is not regenerated in this
pack. PIN unset on the demo book, as shipped.
