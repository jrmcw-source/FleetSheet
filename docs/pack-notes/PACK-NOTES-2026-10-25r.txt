FleetSheet v1.1 — pack 2026-10-25r (build 'r')
================================================

The facelift. Jason's look at build 'q' (2026-10-25): "Generally
I like the way this build looks, but i do want some minor
tweaks, that are nit-picky, but the kind of thing that's easily
noticeable and intimates amateur." Five items; this is the
face lift that wraps up the build.

1. TODAY TICKER SQUEEZE. The alert strip's rows carry no
   vertical margins and tight leading, on every page the strip
   renders — single spacing, down from double. Measured on the
   Today page (demo book, strip open): 157px before, 104px
   after, same rows. Ticker rules otherwise unchanged.

2. ONE PAGE GEOMETRY. Every form page now opens with the same
   header zone (.fhead): Company block at the left, the
   Customer/Address block in the middle, the form number and
   Date at the right — the number larger and bold — then the
   bar, then the form. The header zone measures a uniform 100px
   tall on every document form, and the line header band lands
   at the same Y (235px at 1366x900) on the invoice, payment,
   credit memo, sale, work order, quote, and change order
   forms alike; before, those bands stood at five different
   heights (296–573px). Billing's sub-tabs start at identical
   positions; Check In/Out, Petty Cash, and Reports start where
   everything else starts.

3. NOTHING BELOW THE FORM + UNIVERSAL VIEW BUTTON. Every form
   page ends with one View button at the BOTTOM LEFT, opening
   the page's own list popup (filterable, sortable, column
   headers, no app header inside); the popup's Refresh, Print,
   and Close stand at its BOTTOM RIGHT. New list fragments:
   Work Orders, Quotes, Sales. Removed from under forms: the
   sales register table, the credit page's Open Credit Memos
   table, the invoice editor's below-the-button-row placement
   of the ready-work pickers (they render inside the form now).
   This supersedes the Oct 20 every-page Done-View provision
   and build 'n''s lone-View-door compromise (playbook,
   buttons.md, dated 2026-10-25).

4. NOTHING ABOVE THE LINE ITEMS. Every field that stood
   between the header zone and the line rows is dispositioned:
   needed fields moved into the form's Job Info popup (same
   names, saved with the form — verified end-to-end for the
   work order's Unit/Type/Charge-to/dates, the invoice's
   Reference Ticket/Terms/Header bar note/Transport, the
   sale's Reference #, the rental ticket's Reference Ticket,
   the credit memo's Invoice Ref); the invoice's decorative
   Job Info preview box and its display-only Contact Name span
   are removed (the live Contact Name is the popup's field);
   the credit memo's Inv Date/Reason/Credit Total moved below
   its line rows. The work order is the worked example: WO #
   larger and bold, customer block up top, the Unit/Type/
   Reference Ticket area deleted.

5. BILLING SETTINGS IN FIVE COLUMNS. Admin → Billing: one
   column per option group — Billing Defaults (with the
   printed-invoice Job Info fieldset), Payment Terms, Line
   Units, Line Items, Compliance Documents — options straight
   down, each column scrollable, no option dropped, the same
   POST contract. The page measures 900px tall at 1366x900
   (was 1137px): it fits the screen without a page scroll, and
   a toggle save was verified end-to-end in the new layout.
